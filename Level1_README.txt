ALIEN GAME - LEVEL 1 "QUIET FARM TOWN"

Open the xenomite folder in Unity Hub with our team version (Unity 6000.6.3f1).

Scene:   Assets/Scenes/Level1.unity
Rebuild: menu Alien Game > Build Level 1 (rebuilds the scene from Assets/Scripts/Level1Data.cs)

Play controls
  WASD / arrow keys  move the alien
  SPACE              befriend a close person (placeholder until the brain waves are made)
  R                  restart the level

What is in the level
  - 40 x 26 tile map made with the Tiny Tile Town tiles
  - UFO start in the corn field, 2 tutorial signs
  - 20 civilians (Town Folk sprites). They walk around, and when the alien gets close
    they run along the roads to the closest exit
  - Only 2 exits: Exit A (east bridge) and Exit B (south road)
  - Friendship meter: 10 friends x 10 EXP = 100 EXP. If 11 people escape, restart.
