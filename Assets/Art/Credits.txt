ART CREDITS (put these in the game credits)

All assets were found or made by Talia (team member).

- Tiny Tile Town by Aleksandr Makarov (@IknowKingRabbit)
  Free to use and change. The license asks that he is credited in the game credits,
  and that the asset pack is not hosted publicly except as part of a game. So the repo only
  keeps the pieces the game uses (Art/Level1Pieces); the original pack is not included.
  Get the full pack from the author if you need more pieces.

- Town Folk Sprites by freezepops (itch.io) - CC0 (public domain, no credit needed).

Art/Level1Pieces:
  Pieces cut out of the two packs above for Level 1.
  sign, bridge, exit_marker and heart are small extra sprites made for this level.
  alien_placeholder is a darkened Town Folk villager, used until our alien sprite is ready.

Art/Military:
  Military base sprites (buildings, vehicles, yard tiles, fences) for Level 2,
  cut from one sheet (military.png). Made by Talia (team member).

Art/Level2Pieces:
  folk_police, folk_soldier, folk_noble-lady, folk_king: recoloured Town Folk sprites.
  street_lamp, flood_light and road_00-15 (asphalt) were made for the Level 2 night
  by Tools/make_level2_art.py (the roads reuse the shapes of the Level 1 dirt roads).

Art/Level3Pieces:
  folk_general: the General (Level 3 mini boss), a recolour of folk_soldier, made by
  Tools/make_level3_art.py (which also makes the Psi Shield icon in Resources/Upgrades).

Art/Modern:
  Present-day people and props so every level fits one modern theme: soldier with a tranquilliser
  rifle (folk_trooper/2), police (folk_cop), base staff (clerk, cook, medic, mechanic, pilot),
  townsfolk (watchman, councillor, lineman, gardener), gravestone, flagpole, barrier, helipad;
  plus the General in Level3Pieces. Drawn by Tools/make_modern_art.py in the same style as the town folk.

Art/Alien:
  alien_idle_0/1 - green slime alien (2-frame idle bounce). Found by Talia.
